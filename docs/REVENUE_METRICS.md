## 1. Formulas & Revenue Definitions

1. **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**:
   Sum of all active recurring subscription prices normalized to a monthly rate.
   `MRR = (Monthly Subscriptions) + (Annual Subscriptions / 12)`

2. **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)**:
   `ARR = MRR × 12`

3. **Average Revenue Per Property (ARPU)**:
   `ARPU = MRR / Total Active Paying Properties`

4. **Monthly Churn Rate**:
   `Churn % = (Cancelled Subscriptions in Month / Total Subscriptions at Month Start) × 100`

5. **Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate**:
   `Conversion % = (Converted Paid Subscriptions / Total Expired Trials) × 100`
