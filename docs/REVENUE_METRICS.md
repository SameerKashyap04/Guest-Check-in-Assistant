# Revenue Metrics Specification

## Financial KPIs Defined

- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**: Sum of all active monthly subscription fees + (yearly subscription fees / 12).
- **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)**: MRR × 12.
- **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)**: Total MRR / Number of active paid properties.
- **Paid Conversion Ratio**: (Paid Properties / Total Registered Properties) × 100%.
- **Churn Rate**: (Cancelled Subscriptions in Month / Total Active Paid Subscriptions at Start of Month) × 100%.
